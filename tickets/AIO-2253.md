# T8. **(WARNING fix)** In

`aion/lib/features/tickets/data/daos/ticket_link_dao.dart`,
`getLinksForTicket`'s concatenated `[...asSource, ...asTarget]`
double-counted a self-link (a ticket linked to itself), since such
a row matches both queries' `WHERE` independently — nothing in the
stack actually prevents a self-link from being created (no
`LinksCubit` exists to reject one, and repositories/DAOs are
intentionally unvalidated). Fixed by deduplicating the concatenated
result by `TicketLinkData.id` via a `Map<String, TicketLinkData>`.
Dartdoc updated to state the dedup and why. `design.md`'s "Why two
joined queries" section corrected — the claim that "no
deduplication is needed" is replaced with a new "Why the
concatenated result needs deduplication" section explaining the
actual overlap case and the fix. Added case 6 to `design.md`'s test
plan and a matching test,
`'a self-link (source and target the same ticket) is only counted
once'`, in
`aion/test/features/tickets/data/repositories/drift_ticket_repository_test.dart`'s
`getLinksForTicket excludes trashed` group.
