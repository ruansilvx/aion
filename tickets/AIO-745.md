# Design System Restructure

# Proposal: design-system-restructure

## What

Give the design system its own top-level directory, `lib/design_system/`,
sibling to `lib/core/` and `lib/features/`, organized with standard
atomic-design vocabulary:

- `design_system/tokens/` — the six files currently in `core/theme/`
  (`aion_colors`, `aion_radius`, `aion_shadows`, `aion_text`,
  `aion_theme`, `theme_scope`), moved as-is.
- `design_system/atoms/` — the four smallest, dependency-free widgets:
  `app_button`, `app_checkbox`, `app_text_field`, `app_spinner`.
- `design_system/molecules/` — the five widgets that compose atoms
  and/or manage their own `Overlay`: `app_confirm_dialog`,
  `app_dropdown`, `app_header`, `app_toast`, `selection_menu` — plus
  `InlineEditableField`, promoted from
  `features/tickets/presentation/widgets/inline_editable_field.dart`
  (see below).
- `design_system/design_system.dart` — one barrel exporting all three
  subfolders, replacing today's `core/design_system.dart`.

`core/core.dart` shrinks to what's actually infrastructure:
`database/`, `localization/`, `routing/`, `utils/`. No behavior change
anywhere — this is a pure structural move plus import-path updates.

## Why

Two problems found during `/explore`:

1. **The design system is split across two unrelated barrels for no
   reason tied to actual usage.** `core/theme/` (tokens) and
   `core/widgets/` (components) are siblings under `core/`, exported by
   two separate barrels (`design_system.dart` and `core.dart`) — a
   split the `barrel-files` change deliberately introduced on the
   assumption that some consumers would want tokens without widgets or
   vice versa. Checking actual imports shows that assumption doesn't
   hold: every presentation-layer file that imports one already imports
   the other (10/10), and the only files that import `core.dart`
   *without* tokens are the data-layer DAOs/repositories, which only
   want `AppDatabase` and never touch widgets either. Meanwhile
   `core.dart` bundles the widget library together with `database/`,
   `routing/`, `localization/`, `utils/` — infrastructure that has
   nothing to do with UI. Giving the design system its own directory
   and barrel makes the grouping match how it's actually consumed, and
   leaves `core.dart` meaning what its name says: core infrastructure.

2. **`InlineEditableField` is a generic, feature-agnostic widget stuck
   inside a feature folder.** It takes no `tickets` domain type, no
   `tickets`-specific copy — just `displayText`/`editText`/`parser`/
   `onCommit`/`placeholder`, and it's built entirely from other
   design-system pieces (`AppTextField`, `AppButton`, `AppToast`). Its
   own docstring already says it's reused for four different fields on
   one screen. It meets `project.md`'s Pattern 2 bar for promotion
   ("genuinely generic and reusable, not deeply tied to the owning
   feature's business logic") and belongs in `design_system/molecules/`
   alongside the other overlay-driven composite widgets.

Everything else found in `features/tickets/` during `/explore`
(`PriorityBadge`, `TypeChip`, `StatusIndicator`, `TicketOverflowMenu`,
`TicketSelectionBar`, `TrashedTicketTile`, `LinkCountLabel`, `AppFab`)
takes a `Ticket`/`TicketStatus`/`TicketLinkRepository` param or bakes in
`ticket`-prefixed l10n copy — genuinely feature-coupled, and explicitly
**not** moved by this change. `AppFab` in particular is named like a
design-system atom but hardcodes ticket-creation copy; renaming or
generalizing it is out of scope here (see "Standing convention" below).

## Standing convention (documentation-only, no code change)

Add to `flutter-conventions.md`: a widget must be generalized —
no feature entity/enum constructor params, no feature-specific copy
baked into its body — **before** it's promoted to `design_system/`, not
moved as-is and generalized later. This makes explicit the bar
`InlineEditableField` already clears and `AppFab` currently doesn't.

## Scope

In scope: directory move, barrel rewrite, import-path updates across
every affected file, `project.md`/`flutter-conventions.md` updates
documenting the new directory and the promotion convention.

Out of scope: renaming or generalizing `AppFab`; touching
`PriorityBadge`/`TypeChip`/`StatusIndicator` or any other
feature-coupled widget found during `/explore`; any visual or
behavioral change to any widget.
