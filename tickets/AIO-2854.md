private `_rootPath` getter to read
`widget.project.ticketsGitRootPath` instead of `widget.project.rootPath`
-- this alone repoints `GitProjectingTicketRepository`,

that one local. Separately update the two standalone
`_activeProject(context).rootPath` call sites outside that State class
(the `/workspace/tickets/trash` route's `TrashCubit` and
`/workspace/inbox` route's `InboxCubit`) to
`_activeProject(context).ticketsGitRootPath`. Depends on the previous
