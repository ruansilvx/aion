# Trash Age And Manual Purge

# Proposal: trash-age-and-manual-purge

## What

Two additions to the existing Trash feature:

1. **Age indicator** — each row in `TrashScreen` shows how long a ticket
   has been in Trash (e.g. "Trashed 12 days ago"), reading the
   `deletedAt` timestamp that's already fetched but currently unused in
   the UI.
2. **Manual purge action** — a new "Purge old" button in `TrashScreen`'s
   header that permanently deletes every trashed ticket older than a
   fixed 30-day threshold, after a destructive-toned confirm dialog
   stating the exact count. User-triggered only; nothing runs
   automatically or in the background.

## Why

`aion-arch/specs/tickets.md`'s Known Gaps section flags:

> "No automatic/timed purge of Trash (e.g. auto-delete after 30 days) —
> Trash is manual-only: items stay until the user restores or
> permanently deletes them."

`/explore automatic timed purge trash` investigated closing this gap
with a true timed/background purge and found the app has **no
scheduling infrastructure at all** — no `Timer.periodic`, no
`workmanager`/`BGTaskScheduler`/equivalent, no app-lifecycle hook beyond
a brightness listener in `main.dart`, and no scheduling package in
`pubspec.yaml`. Building that from scratch would mean new
platform-specific integrations per target (Android/iOS/desktop), none
of which exist today, and web has no equivalent at all — trash there
would never auto-purge regardless of the work invested. For a solo-dev,
local-first, no-backend personal tool, that's a lot of new
infrastructure and platform-specific surface area for a low-stakes
feature.

This change delivers the practical benefit — trash stops accumulating
silently forever — without any of that cost, and without the higher
data-loss risk of tickets disappearing on their own with the app closed
and no one watching. It's a deliberately smaller slice than full
automatic purge, chosen after weighing both options (see the `/explore`
session that preceded this proposal).

## Scope

- `TicketRepository.purgeTrashOlderThan(Duration age)` — one new
  repository method, implemented in `DriftTicketRepository` by reusing
  the existing `emptyTrash()` cascade-delete pattern, filtered by age.
  No new DAO methods, no schema changes.
- A new pure formatting utility, `core/utils/relative_time_format.dart`,
  for the age label — mirrors the existing
  `core/utils/duration_format.dart` precedent (a short, unlocalized
  label consumed directly by presentation-layer `Text` widgets, same as
  `formatDurationMinutes` already is in `ticket_detail_screen.dart`).
- `TrashCubit` gains a fixed `purgeAgeThreshold` constant (30 days), a
  `purgeOldTrash()` method, and computes a `purgeEligibleCount` during
  `load()`.
- `TrashedTicketTile` and `TrashScreen` get the two new pieces of UI.
- New `l10n` keys for the purge action's button label, confirm-dialog
  title, and confirm-dialog message.

## Non-goals

- No automatic or background purge — this stays entirely user-triggered.
- No configurable retention period. The 30-day threshold is fixed
  (matching the illustrative figure already used in the spec's Known
  Gaps bullet), not exposed as a setting. A future change could promote
  it to one if that turns out to matter in practice.
- No new Settings UI.
