# Spec Delta: Ticket Trash & Bulk Delete

**Reconciled 2026-09-03:** this delta is the origin of the current trash mechanism and matches `aion-arch/specs/tickets.md`'s "Trash & bulk delete" section closely (schema, `TicketDao`/`DriftTicketRepository` methods, the cascade invariant). Two later changes refined it further without altering this delta's own claims: automatic age-based purging (`purgeTrashOlderThan`, `TrashCubit.purgeAgeThreshold` — see trash-age-and-manual-purge's spec ticket) and restore consolidation through `TicketParentTrashService` (see trash-restore-git-projection's spec ticket). No contradiction, just later additions.

Delta against `aion-arch/specs/tickets.md`. On `/archive`, these changes
merge into that file's prose (not appended as a changelog).

## ADDED

### Data

- `deletedAt` column on `TicketsTable` (schema version 3, nullable Unix
  milliseconds) — `null` means live, non-null means trashed.
- `TicketDao`: `getDescendantIds`, `getAncestorIds`, `softDeleteByIds`,
  `restoreByIds`, `getTrashedTickets`.
- `DriftTicketRepository`: `trashTicket`, `trashTickets`,
  `restoreTicket`, `permanentlyDeleteTicket`, `emptyTrash`,
  `getTrashedTickets` — see design.md §4 for the cascade behavior and
  the "live ticket never has a trashed ancestor" invariant these
  maintain.

### Domain

- `TicketRepository`: `trashTicket(String id)`, `trashTickets(List<String>
  ids) → Future<int>` (returns total tickets actually moved, including
  cascaded descendants), `restoreTicket(String id)`,
  `permanentlyDeleteTicket(String id)`, `emptyTrash()`,
  `getTrashedTickets()`.
- `Ticket` entity gains a nullable `deletedAt` field.

### Presentation

- `TicketsState`: `TicketTrashing`, `TicketTrashed`,
  `TicketsBatchTrashing`, `TicketsBatchTrashed` (carries the refreshed
  list and the actual trashed count).
- `TicketsCubit`: `previewTrashCount(List<String> ids) → Future<int>`
  (cascade preview, no state emitted), `trashTicket(String id)`,
  `trashTickets(List<String> ids)`.
- `TicketSelectionState`/`TicketSelectionCubit` (new, screen-scoped,
  provided via `BlocProvider` around `TicketsListScreen` in
  `app_router.dart`): `isActive` + `selectedIds`, with `enter()`,
  `toggle(id)`, `selectAll(ids)`, `clear()`.
- `TrashState`/`TrashCubit` (new, screen-scoped, provided around the new
  `/tickets/trash` route): `load()`, `restore(id)`,
  `permanentlyDelete(id)`, `emptyTrash()`.
- "Select" toggle icon button and a Trash-entry icon button in
  `TicketsListScreen`'s header.
- `TicketSelectionBar` (new widget) — replaces the create-ticket FAB
  while selection mode is active: selected count, select-all/
  deselect-all toggle, Cancel, Delete (trashes the whole selection,
  cascading).
- `TicketListTile` and `TicketBoardCard`/`_CardVisual` gain a
  selection-mode rendering: a leading `AppCheckbox`, tap-to-toggle
  instead of tap-to-navigate, and `TicketOverflowMenu`/`MoveToStatusMenu`/
  drag suppressed while active.
- `TrashScreen` (new screen, route `/tickets/trash`) and
  `TrashedTicketTile` (new row widget) — lists trashed tickets with
  per-row Restore/Permanently-Delete actions and a screen-level "Empty
  trash" action.

### Core widgets

- `AppCheckbox` (`core/widgets/`) — new non-Material checkbox
  primitive.

### Localization

New `app_en.arb` keys: `ticketTrashConfirmMessage`,
`ticketBulkTrashConfirmTitle`, `ticketBulkTrashSummaryToast`,
`ticketTrashEntryLabel`, `ticketTrashScreenTitle`,
`ticketTrashEmptyState`, `ticketTrashRestoreAction`,
`ticketTrashPermanentDeleteAction`,
`ticketTrashPermanentDeleteConfirmTitle`,
`ticketTrashPermanentDeleteConfirmMessage`, `ticketTrashEmptyAction`,
plus the selection-mode keys (`ticketSelectionToggleLabel`,
`ticketSelectionExitLabel`, `ticketSelectionCountLabel`,
`ticketSelectionSelectAllAction`, `ticketSelectionDeselectAllAction`,
`ticketSelectionDeleteAction`).

## MODIFIED

- `TicketRepository.getAllTickets()` / `searchTickets(...)` — now
  exclude trashed tickets (`deletedAt == null` only). Previously
  returned every ticket regardless.
- `TicketsCubit.getValidParentCandidates` — unchanged code, but its
  behavior changes as a consequence of `getAllTickets()`'s filter: a
  trashed ticket can no longer be offered as a reparent target.
- `TicketOverflowMenu`'s "Delete ticket" action — now calls
  `TicketsCubit.trashTicket` instead of the removed `deleteTicket`, and
  shows a cascade-aware, non-catastrophic confirm message
  (`ticketTrashConfirmMessage`) instead of "This cannot be undone."
- `TicketDao.getAllTickets()` / `searchTickets(...)` — add a `deleted_at
  IS NULL` condition.
- `CommentDao.deleteCommentsForTicket(String)` /
  `TicketLinkDao.deleteLinksForTicket(String)` — generalized to
  `deleteCommentsForTickets(List<String>)` /
  `deleteLinksForTickets(List<String>)` (batch, called with a whole
  subtree's ids at once instead of one ticket at a time).
- `TicketDao.deleteTicketRow(String)` — replaced by
  `deleteTicketRows(List<String>)` (batch).

## REMOVED

- `TicketRepository.deleteTicket` — replaced by `trashTicket` (soft) and
  `permanentlyDeleteTicket` (hard, trash-only).
- `TicketHasChildrenException` (domain/exceptions) — deleted outright;
  nothing blocks on structural children anymore.
- `TicketDao.countChildTickets` — unused after the block-on-children
  behavior it existed solely to support was removed.
- `TicketsState`: `TicketDeleting`, `TicketDeleted` — replaced by
  `TicketTrashing`, `TicketTrashed`.
- `TicketsErrorReason.hasChildren`, `TicketsError.childCount` — nothing
  classifies or carries this anymore.
- `app_en.arb` keys: `ticketDeleteConfirmMessage` (replaced by
  `ticketTrashConfirmMessage`), `ticketDeleteBlockedByChildren`,
  `ticketDeleteBlockedByChildrenGeneric`.

## Known gaps closed (remove from `tickets.md`'s Known Gaps list on `/archive`)

- "Deletion is single-ticket and permanent — no bulk/multi-select
  delete, no undo/trash/soft-delete." Both halves closed together.

## Unaffected (explicitly, to prevent scope creep during `/apply`)

- No automatic/timed purge of trash — manual restore/permanent-delete/
  empty-trash only. A future gap, not this change's job.
- No multi-select inside the Trash screen itself beyond "Empty trash."
- No bulk operations other than delete (status change, reparent, type/
  priority edit).
- No change to how `ticket_links`/`LinkCountLabel` account for a
  currently-trashed linked ticket.
- No selection or bulk-delete affordance on `TicketDetailScreen` — it
  keeps using `TicketOverflowMenu`'s single-ticket `trashTicket` path,
  unchanged in shape (just renamed from `deleteTicket`).
