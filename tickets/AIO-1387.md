(finding #3): promoted the existing `TypeChip`/`ticketTypeLabel`
(previously private to `tickets_list_screen.dart`/
`tickets_board_view.dart`) to `design_system/molecules/type_chip.dart`
per Pattern 2 — it was already generic (`TicketType` + `isRow` bool,

sync-status badge, and delete action — the title editor moved out of

/ `tickets_board_view.dart` and their two other consumers
(`ticket_link_picker.dart`, `trashed_ticket_tile.dart`) to the promoted
import; no behavior change for `TicketDetailScreen` itself.
