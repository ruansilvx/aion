### Post-`/opsx-verify` fixes

`/opsx-verify` found that `MoveToStatusMenu` and `_ViewModeToggle`'s
`_ViewModeIcon` were wrapped only in `Semantics(button: true)` +
`GestureDetector`, with no `FocusableActionDetector`/`ActivateIntent` —
unlike every other interactive primitive in the codebase (`AppButton`,
`AppFab`, `AppDropdown`, `TicketListTile`, `TicketBoardCard`). This meant
neither was reachable via Tab or activatable via Enter/Space, directly
contradicting design.md's explicit claim that `MoveToStatusMenu` is "the
keyboard/screen-reader-accessible equivalent" of dragging. Also found:
`TicketsCubit.createTicket`'s current-list `switch` wasn't extended to
read from the two states this change added
(`TicketStatusUpdating`/`TicketStatusUpdated`), unlike
`updateTicketStatus`'s switch — creating a possible one-frame flash to an
empty list if a ticket is created immediately after a drag.
